Direct support from the development team and official channels.

## Issue Tracking

**GitHub Issues**: [github.com/struct-mark-docs/issues](https://github.com/struct-mark-docs/issues)

**Issue Categories**:
- **Bug Reports**: Problems and unexpected behavior
- **Feature Requests**: New functionality suggestions
- **Documentation Issues**: Errors or improvements in docs
- **Performance Issues**: Speed and resource concerns

**Reporting Guidelines**:
1. **Search First**: Check if issue already exists
2. **Use Templates**: Follow provided issue templates
3. **Provide Details**: Include system info and reproduction steps
4. **Be Specific**: Clear, detailed descriptions help resolution

**Issue Priority**:
- **Critical**: Data loss, security, system crashes
- **High**: Major functionality broken
- **Medium**: Workarounds available
- **Low**: Minor inconveniences or enhancements

## Support Guidelines

### Getting Help Effectively

1. **Be Specific**: Provide detailed information about your issue
2. **Include Context**: Share system version, configuration, and environment
3. **Show Your Work**: Include what you've already tried
4. **Be Patient**: Allow time for community responses

### Giving Help Effectively

1. **Ask Clarifying Questions**: Ensure you understand the issue
2. **Provide Step-by-Step Solutions**: Clear, actionable instructions
3. **Explain the Why**: Help users understand underlying concepts
4. **Follow Up**: Check if your solution worked

### Community Standards

- **Be Respectful**: Treat all community members with respect
- **Be Inclusive**: Welcome users of all skill levels and backgrounds
- **Be Constructive**: Focus on solutions rather than criticism
- **Be Professional**: Maintain professional communication standards
