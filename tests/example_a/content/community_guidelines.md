# Code of Conduct

Our community is built on respect, collaboration, and mutual support. These guidelines help ensure a positive experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

### Respect and Inclusivity
- **Be Respectful**: Treat all community members with dignity and respect
- **Be Inclusive**: Welcome participants of all backgrounds and skill levels
- **Be Patient**: Understand that everyone has different learning curves
- **Be Supportive**: Help others learn and grow

### Constructive Participation
- **Stay On Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the channel or thread
- **Provide Value**: Share knowledge and experiences that help others
- **Acknowledge Mistakes**: Admit errors and learn from them
- **Give Credit**: Recognize others' contributions and ideas

### Professional Communication
- **Use Clear Language**: Communicate clearly and avoid unnecessary jargon
- **Be Professional**: Maintain professional tone in all interactions
- **Listen Actively**: Consider others' perspectives before responding
- **Assume Good Intent**: Give others the benefit of the doubt

## Unacceptable Behavior

### Harassment and Discrimination
- **Personal Attacks**: No personal insults, harassment, or bullying
- **Discrimination**: No discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or background
- **Threats**: No threats of violence or harm
- **Privacy Violations**: No sharing of private information without consent

### Disruptive Behavior
- **Spam**: No unsolicited commercial content or repetitive messages
- **Trolling**: No deliberate attempts to provoke or disrupt discussions
- **Off-Topic Content**: No irrelevant content that derails productive discussions
- **Abuse of Power**: No using positions of influence to intimidate or control

### Professional Boundaries
- **Plagiarism**: No claiming others' work as your own
- **Confidential Information**: No sharing of proprietary or confidential information
- **Legal Violations**: No encouraging or facilitating illegal activities
- **Manipulation**: No attempts to manipulate community decisions or processes

# Contribution Standards

High standards ensure quality and maintainability of project contributions.

## Code Contributions

### Quality Requirements
- **Test Coverage**: All code must include comprehensive tests
- **Documentation**: New features require appropriate documentation
- **Style Consistency**: Follow project coding standards and conventions
- **Performance**: Consider performance implications of changes

### Submission Process
1. **Create Issue**: Discuss major changes in an issue first
2. **Fork Repository**: Create a personal fork for development
3. **Branch Strategy**: Use descriptive branch names for features
4. **Commit Messages**: Write clear, descriptive commit messages
5. **Pull Request**: Submit PR with clear description and testing instructions

### Review Guidelines
- **Constructive Feedback**: Provide helpful, specific feedback
- **Testing Requirements**: Ensure all tests pass before merge
- **Documentation Updates**: Update relevant documentation with changes
- **Backward Compatibility**: Consider impact on existing users

## Documentation Contributions

### Content Standards
- **Accuracy**: Ensure all information is technically accurate
- **Clarity**: Write clearly and concisely for target audience
- **Completeness**: Provide comprehensive coverage of topics
- **Current**: Keep content up to date with latest features

### Style Guidelines
- **Voice**: Maintain consistent, professional tone
- **Format**: Follow established formatting and structure
- **Examples**: Include practical, tested examples
- **Accessibility**: Ensure content is accessible to all users

### Submission Process
1. **Check Existing**: Review current documentation to avoid duplication
2. **Follow Templates**: Use established templates for consistency
3. **Get Review**: Submit for community review and feedback
4. **Iterate**: Refine based on reviewer suggestions
5. **Final Approval**: Wait for maintainer approval before publication

# Communication Protocols

Effective communication helps build a strong, supportive community.

## Forum Etiquette

### Asking Questions
- **Search First**: Check existing discussions for similar questions
- **Be Specific**: Provide detailed information about your issue
- **Include Context**: Share system version, configuration, and environment
- **Follow Up**: Respond to questions and provide additional information

### Answering Questions
- **Be Helpful**: Provide clear, actionable solutions
- **Explain Reasoning**: Help others understand why solutions work
- **Acknowledge Limits**: Be honest about what you don't know
- **Encourage Others**: Invite additional perspectives and solutions

### Discussion Participation
- **Stay Relevant**: Keep contributions on topic and productive
- **Build on Ideas**: Extend and improve others' suggestions
- **Disagree Respectfully**: Challenge ideas, not people
- **Know When to Stop**: Recognize when discussions have reached conclusions

## Chat Guidelines

### Real-Time Communication
- **Be Patient**: Allow time for responses in real-time discussions
- **Take Turns**: Avoid interrupting or dominating conversations
- **Use Threads**: Keep related messages together in threads
- **Summarize**: Help summarize key points for late arrivals

### Professional Conduct
- **Appropriate Channels**: Use correct channels for different topics
- **Time Zone Awareness**: Consider global community and time differences
- **Professional Language**: Maintain professionalism even in casual settings
- **Privacy Respect**: Keep private conversations private

### Conflict Resolution
- **Address Privately**: Handle disagreements through private messages when appropriate
- **Seek Mediation**: Ask moderators for help with persistent conflicts
- **Focus on Issues**: Address problems, not personalities
- **Move Forward**: Know when to agree to disagree

# Community Governance

How our community is organized and decisions are made.

## Leadership Structure

### Core Team
- **Project Maintainers**: Responsible for technical direction and code reviews
- **Community Managers**: Handle community operations and user support
- **Documentation Leads**: Manage documentation quality and organization
- **Moderation Team**: Enforce community guidelines and handle conflicts

### Community Roles
- **Contributors**: Active participants who regularly contribute to the project
- **Ambassadors**: Community advocates who help and represent the project
- **Mentors**: Experienced users who help newcomers learn
- **Reviewers**: Trusted contributors who help review submissions

## Decision Making

### Technical Decisions
- **Proposal Process**: Major changes proposed through issues or RFCs
- **Community Discussion**: Open discussion period for feedback
- **Consensus Building**: Seek broad agreement before implementation
- **Final Authority**: Project maintainers make final decisions

### Community Policies
- **Guideline Updates**: Community input on guideline changes
- **Transparency**: Public discussion of policy changes
- **Gradual Implementation**: Phased rollout of significant changes
- **Feedback Mechanisms**: Ongoing collection of community feedback

## Enforcement

### Reporting Process
1. **Direct Contact**: Address issues directly when appropriate and safe
2. **Moderator Report**: Contact moderators for serious issues
3. **Formal Complaint**: Use official channels for formal complaints
4. **Documentation**: Document incidents for follow-up and patterns

### Response Procedures
- **Immediate Action**: Address urgent issues quickly
- **Investigation**: Thoroughly investigate all reports
- **Proportionate Response**: Apply consequences appropriate to the situation
- **Community Communication**: Communicate actions to maintain trust

### Consequences
- **Warning**: Private warning for first or minor offenses
- **Temporary Suspension**: Temporary removal from community spaces
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal for serious or repeated offenses
- **Legal Action**: Involvement of law enforcement for illegal activities

## Community Health

### Monitoring and Improvement
- **Regular Reviews**: Periodic review of community health and guidelines
- **Feedback Collection**: Ongoing collection of community feedback
- **Metrics Tracking**: Monitor participation and satisfaction metrics
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regular updates to improve community experience

### Support Resources
- **Mental Health**: Resources for community mental health and well-being
- **Conflict Resolution**: Mediation and conflict resolution services
- **Training**: Resources for community leadership and communication skills
- **Recognition**: Programs to recognize and appreciate community contributions

### Celebrating Success
- **Contributor Spotlight**: Regular recognition of valuable contributors
- **Milestone Celebrations**: Community celebrations of project achievements
- **Success Stories**: Sharing of community success stories and case studies
- **Annual Awards**: Recognition of outstanding community members

## Getting Involved

### Start Small
- **Lurk and Learn**: Observe community interactions before participating
- **Ask Questions**: Don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification
- **Share Experiences**: Contribute your unique perspective and experiences
- **Help Others**: Answer questions when you have knowledge to share

### Grow Your Impact
- **Take Initiative**: Propose improvements and new ideas
- **Lead Projects**: Organize community projects or initiatives
- **Mentor Others**: Help newcomers learn and get involved
- **Represent Community**: Become a community ambassador or advocate

### Long-term Participation
- **Stay Engaged**: Maintain regular, constructive participation
- **Evolve with Community**: Adapt to community growth and changes
- **Give Back**: Contribute back to the community that supports you
- **Build Relationships**: Develop meaningful connections with community members
